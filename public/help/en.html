Padel One Player

Help Center

Guide for the player app you see on padel1.app — the home screen with four shortcuts.

This page only covers what players see. Court booking, academy and “find a game” appear only if a club has the full manager version — which many players do not have.

The 4 shortcuts

  • Rankings — club / global 12‑month standings, or by ELO level; men/women filter.
  • Tournaments — enter events, see draws and standings.
  • Results — your match history.
  • Register result — log a match already played (4 players + sets).

Rankings

12‑month points from all tournaments and validated games, or sort by current ELO. Club ranking is titled with your club’s name. Older points drop off after 365 days automatically.

Points by tournament

  • Master / Official — 1st 1000 · 2nd 600 · 3rd/4th 360 · quarters 180 · groups 50
  • Regular / Open — 1st 500 · 2nd 300 · 3rd/4th 180 · quarters 90 · groups 25
  • American / Social — 1st 250 · 2nd 150 · 3rd/4th 90 · quarters 45 · groups 15
  • Validated casual games — win +15 · loss +5

Tournaments

Register, follow matches and standings. Organizer results update your ELO and 12‑month ranking.

Results

Your W/L history and recent matches from tournaments and registered games.

Register result

Pick club, date, 4 players and sets. ELO updates for everyone; opponents can dispute. Validated games also add ranking points (+15 win / +5 loss).

ELO level

Level ~0.5–7.0+ after each rated match. New players move faster; tournaments keep a minimum K so veterans don’t stall. Final: champion +0.10, finalist +0.05. Category labels (M3/F4) are not your ELO.

Account

Login = phone + password (not email). Phone is your identity. Edit profile, photo and bio from the profile screen.
